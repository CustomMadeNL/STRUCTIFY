# STRUCTIFY — Policies & FAQ (Etsy + Payhip)

> Shop-wide policy and FAQ copy, ready to paste into Etsy shop policies, Payhip
> store settings, and the FAQ section of each product page. Written for **digital
> download** products.
>
> ⚠️ **Legal note:** this is practical, plain-language copy — not legal advice.
> STRUCTIFY / Custommade Agency is EU-based, so EU consumer rules for digital
> content apply. Have your final policies checked against Etsy's and Payhip's
> current requirements and applicable consumer law before publishing.

---

## 1. Refund & returns policy

**Short version (product-page line):**

```
Because this is an instant digital download, all sales are final — no returns,
exchanges or cancellations once the files have been delivered. If you have any
problem accessing your product, email info@custommade.agency and we'll make it right.
```

**Full policy (shop settings):**

```
DIGITAL PRODUCTS — NO RETURNS

All STRUCTIFY products are digital downloads (Google Sheets templates and PDF
guides). Because the files are delivered instantly and cannot be "returned," we
do not offer refunds, returns, exchanges or cancellations once your order is
delivered and the files are accessible.

EU RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL

For customers in the EU: digital content supplied immediately is exempt from the
14-day right of withdrawal once delivery has started with your consent. By
completing your purchase and accessing the files, you agree to immediate delivery
and acknowledge that your right of withdrawal no longer applies.

WE STILL HELP YOU

A "no refund" policy is not a "no support" policy. If a link is broken, a file
won't open, or you can't access what you paid for, email info@custommade.agency
with your order number and we will fix it quickly — replacement file, working
link, or a resend.

EXCEPTIONS

We may, at our discretion, offer a refund or replacement if:
- The product was not delivered and we cannot resolve the access issue, or
- The product is materially not as described.
```

## 3. Product-page FAQ block

Paste into the FAQ / description section of each Etsy and Payhip product page.

```
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is this a physical product?
No. This is a 100% digital product. Nothing will be shipped to you.

What do I receive after purchase?
A delivery guide (PDF) containing links to every file, plus simple setup
instructions. Open the guide first — it tells you exactly where to start.

Do I need any special software?
You need a free Google account to use the Google Sheets. That's it. No paid apps,
no subscriptions.

Can I edit the files?
Yes. Open each Google Sheet and go to File > Make a copy to create your own
editable version. Always work in your copy, never the original.

Do I get instant access?
Yes. The download is available immediately after purchase.

Can I use this for my business?
Yes — personal and business use are both allowed. You just can't resell or share
the files themselves.

Can I get a refund?
Because this is an instant digital download, all sales are final. But if you can't
access your product, email info@custommade.agency and we'll fix it fast.

Will I get updates?
When we improve a product (better prompts, new modules, refined templates),
verified buyers can access the update. Keep your delivery guide — it's your access
point.

I have a problem — how do I get help?
Email info@custommade.agency with your product name, order number and a short
description of the issue (a screenshot helps). We'll get you sorted.
```
