# STRUCTIFY — Brand

## Tagline

**Simplify. Structure. Scale.**

Alternative line used on delivery assets: _Systems for a Better Life._

## Positioning

Systems, templates and AI tools to organize life, work, goals and business.
STRUCTIFY sits at the intersection of productivity, AI tools, business operations
and everyday life organization.

## Brand personality

- Clean
- Practical
- Premium
- Modern
- Useful

Explicitly **not**: childish, overdesigned, complicated.

## Voice

- Direct and calm. Short sentences. Outcome-first.
- Speak to one bottleneck at a time. The core principle sold to customers:
  **one goal first, one system first, one workflow first — scale only after the
  first workflow works.**
- No hype, no emoji clutter, no "hustle" language.

## Visual rules

- Palette: **black / white / off-white only.** No random colors.
- Consistent STRUCTIFY typography across all assets.
- Product name and outcome must be readable within 3 seconds.
- Must look premium and marketplace-ready on mobile — never "student-made."

## Sales channels

- **Now:** Etsy, Payhip, Canva templates, Pinterest (traffic).
- **Later:** Gumroad, Shopify, Amazon KDP.

## Product families

1. AI Packs
2. Business Packs
3. Life Organization Packs
4. Focus / ADHD-friendly Packs
5. Family / Household Packs
6. Workflow / Operations Packs
