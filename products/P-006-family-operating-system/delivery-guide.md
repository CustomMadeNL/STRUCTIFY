## Page 6 — Quick start: your first session

1. Open **Start Here**.
2. Make your own copy of each Google Sheet.
3. Set up your **Family Calendar** as the shared hub.
4. Plan this week in the **Meal Planner**.
5. Assign chores in the **Chore System**.

Then add the kids' **Routine** and household **Screen Time** rules, and share the
copies with the family.
