## Page 4 — How to use this product

Always work inside **your own copy**: open a file → **File → Make a copy** → save
to your Drive → edit only your copy. This keeps the originals safe.

**Recommended use:** set up the Life Dashboard first, then add one module at a
time. Review weekly, not once.

## Page 6 — Quick start: your first session

1. Open **Start Here**.
2. Make your own copy of each Google Sheet.
3. Set up your **Life Dashboard**.
4. Add your top goals in the **Goal System**.
5. Plan the week in **Weekly Planning**.

Then add Daily Planning, Habit Tracker, Personal CRM and Energy Management, and use
the Weekly/Monthly Reviews to stay on track.
