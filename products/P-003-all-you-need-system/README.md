# P-003 — All You Need System

**Organize your life, work and goals in one place.**

- **Brand:** STRUCTIFY
- **Category:** Life / Productivity / Digital Operating System
- **Launch priority:** #3 (broadest audience)
- **Status:** Planned — modules defined in the registry (`BUILD` stage). Not launch-ready until modules are finalized.

## Core promise

Templates, workflows and AI prompts to organize your life, work, goals and routines
in one clean system — without the overwhelm of ten different apps.

## Target audience

Everyone who wants life, work and goals organized in one place: professionals,
students, creatives and busy people.

## Customer flow

1. Open **Start Here**.
2. Set up the **Life Dashboard** — see all your areas in one place.
3. Define goals in the **Goal System**.
4. Run **Weekly** and **Daily Planning**.
5. Track habits and energy.
6. Use **Weekly** and **Monthly Review** to stay on course.
