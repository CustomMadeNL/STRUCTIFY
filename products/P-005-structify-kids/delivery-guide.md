to your Drive → edit only your copy.

and simple. Review once a week and adjust — routines change as kids grow.

## Page 6 — Quick start: your first session

1. Open **Start Here** and read the 2-minute setup.
2. Make your own copy of each Google Sheet.
3. Build the **Kids Routine** together with your child.
4. Add this week's tasks to the **Homework System**.
5. Agree the rules in the **Screen Time System**.

Then pick one or two goals in the **Kids Goal Tracker**.
