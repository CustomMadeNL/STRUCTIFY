**Help kids build structure — without the daily battles.**

## Core promise

Simple routines, homework, screen-time and goals in one system parents can set up
in minutes and kids can actually follow.

## Target audience

Parents of school-age children who want calmer mornings, smoother homework and
clearer screen-time — without nagging.

## Customer flow

1. Open **Start Here** (parent setup).
2. Set up the **Kids Routine System** together with your child.
3. Add school tasks to the **Homework System**.
4. Agree screen-time rules in the **Screen Time System**.
5. Pick one or two goals in the **Kids Goal Tracker**.
